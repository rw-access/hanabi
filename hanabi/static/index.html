          How to play

          Build each color from 1 to 5, in order. You and {{teammate}} share a score of up to 25. You only see the other player’s cards.

          Give a clue
          Spend one token to mark every card of a color or number in {{teammate}}’s hand. This table allows empty clues. If there are no 1s, “No 1s” rules out 1 for every card currently in that hand. A replacement card starts fresh. {{teammate_rules}}
          Read your cards
          The suit symbols and numbers on each back show what is still possible. Select a card for the odds of each suit and number together. These use clues, cards you can see, and the number of copies left for your whole hand. A dash means the combination is impossible.
          Play a card
          Pick one of your hidden cards. If it’s the next number in its color, it adds to the stack. A misplaced card goes to the discard pile and costs a fuse. Playing a 5 earns a clue if there’s room.
          Discard
          Reveal and toss a card to regain one clue. You can’t discard when all 8 clue tokens are available. Cards drawn to replace played or discarded cards go on the right.
          Finish
          When the last card is drawn, each player gets one more turn. Completing all five fireworks ends the game early. Lose all 3 fuses and the official score is 0.

          Start over?

          This replaces the current game with a fresh deck.
